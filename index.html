sheet 02 — origin

About

Growing up, I wanted to be a mechanical engineer. I loved the idea of building things that didn't exist yet — machines, inventions, anything that turned an idea into something real and moving.

That curiosity found a new outlet when I started asking how computers and phones actually work — how an idea becomes an app, a website, a game. Chasing that answer is what pulled me into computing, and it still drives how I approach every project: take it apart, understand it, then build it better.

I'm currently a Computer Science undergraduate at the Nigerian Army University, Biu, in the Faculty of Computing — still early in the course, still curious about most of the same things.
